SpyderWash Product Overview
============================

What is SpyderWash?
-------------------
SpyderWash is a cashless payment and management system designed specifically for laundromats and commercial laundry facilities. It is manufactured and supported by Setomatic Systems Inc, a Cantaloupe company. SpyderWash enables laundromat operators to modernize their facilities by replacing coin-operated payment systems with card-based and mobile payment solutions.

Key Components
--------------
- Card Readers: Installed on each washer and dryer, Card Readers accept loyalty cards and process cashless payments. They connect wirelessly to the SpyderWash Hub via Bluetooth.
- Bluetooth Hubs (Smart Hubs): Central connectivity devices that link Card Readers to the internet. Each Hub supports approximately 35-40 machines. Older Hubs have green LEDs; newer Smart Hubs have blue LEDs and offer improved performance.
- Kiosks: Touchscreen terminals where customers can purchase, reload, and manage loyalty cards. Kiosks support credit/debit card payments and dispense loyalty cards.
- Loyalty Cards: Reloadable prepaid cards that customers use to pay for wash and dry cycles. Cards carry a balance and can be tracked through the operator portal.
- SpyderWatch: A real-time monitoring feature that allows operators to view machine status, cycle activity, and revenue remotely.

Features and Capabilities
-------------------------
- Cashless Payments: Accept loyalty card payments on all connected machines, eliminating the need for coin mechanisms.
- Remote Monitoring (SpyderWatch): View real-time machine status, cycle counts, and connectivity status from the operator portal.
- Loyalty Programs: Operators can create bonus programs, promotional funds, and free wash/dry rewards for customers.
- Transaction Management: Full transaction history, revenue reporting, and refund capabilities through the operator portal.
- Multi-Location Support: Manage multiple laundromat locations from a single operator account.
- Revenue Reporting: Detailed reports by location, machine type, time of day, day of week, month, and position.
- Card Issuance: Issue loyalty cards to locations or directly to customers with configurable starting balances.
- Attendant Management: Create employee cards and time clock cards for staff.

Who Uses SpyderWash?
--------------------
SpyderWash is used by laundromat operators who want to offer cashless payment options, monitor their equipment remotely, run loyalty programs, and access detailed revenue analytics. Operators manage their facilities through the SpyderWash Operator Portal at portal.setomaticsystems.com.

Technical Architecture
----------------------
SpyderWash operates on a Bluetooth-to-Internet architecture:
1. Card Readers on machines communicate with the nearest Bluetooth Hub.
2. Hubs connect to the internet via Ethernet through the location's router/modem.
3. Hubs sync machine data with SpyderWash Servers (cloud infrastructure).
4. Operators access data through the web-based Operator Portal or via API integrations.

The system requires a stable internet connection at each location. If a Hub loses connectivity, machines may display "Offline" or "Profile==Default" status until the connection is restored.
