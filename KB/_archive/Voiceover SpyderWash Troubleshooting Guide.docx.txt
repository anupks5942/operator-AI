Voiceover: SpyderWash Troubleshooting Guide



Intro

In this video, we will guide you through the troubleshooting steps for the SpyderWash system.



For convenience, this video is organized into four sections: Power Issues, Connectivity Issues, Configuration Issues, and Post-Installation Issues.







Power Issues

In this section, we identify the most common causes of power-related issues and how to resolve them.



Shortly after the machine is powered on, the Card Reader should illuminate and display “System Initializing…”



If you do not see this message, confirm that the Card Reader is properly connected to the SpyderWash Control Board.



To avoid the risk of injury or damage to equipment, power off the machine.



Connect or reseat the cable from the SpyderWash Card Reader to the Control Board.



Power on the machine and test.



If the issue persists, power off the machine and verify that the barrel plug from the power supply is securely connected to the SpyderWash Control Board.



In most cases, the barrel connector is either disconnected or not fully seated.



Power on the machine and test.



If both the Card Reader and Power Supply are properly connected and the display remains unpowered, measure the voltage output from the barrel connector.



Set your multimeter to DC voltage and ensure the output measures at least 9.25 volts.



If voltage is low or not present, power off the machine and verify that the power supply connections are properly seated and securely fastened.



Power on the machine and test.



If all previous steps have failed to resolve the issue, measure the input voltage to the power supply.



With the multimeter set to AC voltage, place one probe on each terminal and confirm that the input voltage measures at least 115 volts.



If the large power supply has no voltage, confirm that the machine is plugged in and that the power switch is on.



If the voltage is low or continues to measure 0 volts, contact SpyderWash Support for further troubleshooting and assistance.







Connectivity Issues

In this section, we review common connectivity issues and how to resolve them.



As the Card Reader initializes, several key status messages will briefly appear on the display.



The Firmware Version of the Card Reader, the Bluetooth Address, the Hub Data status, the Profile status, and the Firmware Version of the Control Board.



The price will appear once the Card Reader has finished initializing.



If the machine is powered off intentionally or due to power loss, the Control Board will automatically attempt to reconnect to the Hub once power is restored.



While the Card Reader is initializing, you should see Hub Data Default followed by Profile = Acquired.



Profile = Acquired indicates that the Control Board is paired with the Hub and has successfully connected to the Internet.



If you obtain Profile = Default, confirm that the Bluetooth Hub is powered on and that the status lights are illuminated.



If the issue persists, ensure that the Control Board is paired with the Bluetooth Hub.



To pair the Control Board with the Hub, unplug the power cord from the Hub or electrical outlet, then plug it back in.



Wait 90 seconds for the solid blue light on the Hub to begin flashing, then power off the machine.



After a few seconds, power the machine back on and observe the Card Reader during initialization.



Please note that the Bluetooth Hub will remain in Discovery Mode for approximately 7 minutes.



During the pairing process, you should see Hub Data Acquired.



If you are unable to obtain Hub Data Acquired, evaluate the physical placement of the Hub.



The Control Boards require a clear line of sight to the Bluetooth Hub for reliable communication.



If the Hub is obstructed by walls, metal surfaces, or large equipment, reposition it to a more centralized and elevated location.



In larger arcade environments, signal strength may be impacted by layout and machine density.



If a substantial number of SpyderWash kits are installed, additional Hubs may be necessary to ensure adequate coverage.



If you obtain Hub Data Acquired but continue to receive Profile = Default, verify that the Hub is able to connect to the Internet.



The Bluetooth Hub requires a direct internet connection via Ethernet.



In most cases, restarting the network equipment will resolve this issue.



Unplug or power off the modem and/or router for 60 seconds, then power it back on.



Once the device has restarted, unplug the power cord from the Bluetooth Hub or electrical outlet, then plug it back in.



Wait 30 seconds for the Hub to boot, then perform a test transaction.



If the issue persists, confirm with your Internet Service Provider that they are able to successfully ping a device on your network.



In extremely rare circumstances, the Card Reader may exhibit a false-positive message during initialization.



If you observe "Error Attempting Discovery" but obtain "Profile = Acquired," you can safely disregard the error message.



If you consistently obtain "Error Attempting Discovery" followed by Profile = Default, reposition the antenna connected to the SpyderWash Control Board.



The antenna extends the Bluetooth range of the Control Board but may cause interference when installed in close proximity to other antennas.



If the Control Board is paired with a Hub that has an active internet connection and you continue to observe "Profile = Default," note the Bluetooth Address of the Control Board.



The Bluetooth Address is displayed briefly on the Card Reader during initialization.



It can also be found on the label located on the back of the Control Board.



With the Bluetooth Address available, contact SpyderWash Support for further troubleshooting and assistance.







Configuration Issues

In this section, we explore common configuration issues and outline the steps to resolve them.



The price should appear shortly after obtaining Profile = Acquired.



If the price is missing or misconfigured, note the Bluetooth Address of the Control Board.



The Bluetooth Address is displayed on the Card Reader during initialization.



It is also labeled on the back of the Control Board.



With the Bluetooth Address available, please contact SpyderWash Support for further troubleshooting and assistance.







Post-Installation Issues

In this section, we review issues that may arise after installation and outline the steps to resolve them.



If you observe “Offline” on the Card Reader, confirm that the SpyderWash Control Board has been paired with the Bluetooth Hub.



“Offline” indicates that the Control Board has not been paired with the Hub and has not synced with the SpyderWash Servers.



To pair the Control Board with the Hub, unplug the power cord from the Hub or electrical outlet, then plug it back in.



Wait 90 seconds for the solid blue light on the Hub to begin flashing, then power off the machine.



After a few seconds, power the machine back on and observe the Card Reader during initialization.



During the pairing process, you should see Hub Data Acquired followed by the Profile status.



If you are unable to obtain Hub Data Acquired, evaluate the physical placement of the Hub.



The Control Boards require a clear line of sight to the Bluetooth Hub for reliable communication.



If the Hub is obstructed by walls, metal surfaces, or large equipment, reposition it to a more centralized and elevated location.



If a significant number of SpyderWash kits are installed, additional Hubs may be necessary to ensure adequate coverage.



If you receive “No Connection” on multiple or all Card Readers, confirm that the Bluetooth Hub is powered on and that the status lights are illuminated.



“No Connection” indicates that the Control Board was unable to communicate with the Hub.



If the issue persists, ensure that the affected unit is paired with the Bluetooth Hub.



To pair the Control Board with the Hub, unplug the power cord from the Hub or electrical outlet, then plug it back in.



Wait 90 seconds for the solid blue light on the Hub to begin flashing, then power off the machine.



After a few seconds, power the machine back on and observe the Card Reader during initialization.



During the pairing process, you should see Hub Data Acquired followed by the Profile status.



If you are unable to obtain Hub Data Acquired, evaluate the physical placement of the Hub.



If you observe “Network Error” on a single Card Reader while all other units are functioning properly, have the customer reattempt the payment.



In some cases, the unit may briefly lose communication and automatically reconnect during a subsequent transaction attempt.



If you consistently observe “Network Error” on multiple or all Card Readers, confirm that you have an active Internet connection.



“Network Error” indicates that the Control Board is able to communicate with the Bluetooth Hub but was unable to reach the Internet.



In most cases, restarting the network equipment will resolve this issue.



Unplug or power off the modem and/or router for 60 seconds, then power it back on.



Once the device has restarted, unplug the power cord from the Bluetooth Hub or electrical outlet, then plug it back in.



Wait 30 seconds for the Hub to boot, then perform a test transaction.



If the issue persists, confirm with your Internet Service Provider that they are able to successfully ping a device on your network.



If the Control Board is paired with a Hub that has an active internet connection and you continue to observe “Network Error,” note the Bluetooth Address of the Control Board.



The Bluetooth Address is displayed briefly on the Card Reader during initialization.



It can also be found on the label located on the back of the Control Board.



With the Bluetooth Address available, please contact SpyderWash Support for further troubleshooting and assistance.



If the machine does not start after a successful payment attempt, verify that the harness is securely connected to both the SpyderWash Control Board and the machine interface.



If all previous steps have failed to resolve the issue, power off the machine and swap the Control Board with a known working unit.



Power on the machine and test.



If the issue persists, power off the machine, reconnect the original Control Board, and swap the Card Reader with a known working unit.



Power on the machine and test.



If the issue remains unresolved, please contact SpyderWash Support for further troubleshooting and assistance.



This concludes the troubleshooting process for the SpyderWash system.
