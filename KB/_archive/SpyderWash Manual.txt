Steps to setting up the new loyalty program which works for BOTH Credit/Debit Cards and Loyalty Cards.
Please note that the same Card must be used in order to get the promotion. If you have several different
Loyalty Cards and/or Credit Cards, the promotion will be separate for each card and they will not combine.
The cards do not need to be registered to participate in the loyalty program, however if the Loyalty Card or
Credit Card is registered the customer can see all their transactions and how many washes left until they
are eligible from the Free Wash.
•Please note that you can only choose one program (Usage or Points) and if you want to switch loyalty
programs from Usage to Points or vice versa, the loyalty rewards earned till date will not transfer.
However, if you go back to the original program you had, the loyalty rewards earned will still be there.*
